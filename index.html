More Than Just Coffee

The 1997 Coffee and Space is a spacious, community-driven coffee house designed for thinkers, creators, and dreamers. Inspired by 90s nostalgia and modern space aesthetics, we serve more than coffee — we serve experiences.

Every cup tells a story of passion, quality, and dedication. We believe that great coffee brings people together, creates memories, and sparks conversations that matter.
